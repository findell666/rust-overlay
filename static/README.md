# Screenshots

Images used by the documentation. Everything here is referenced from `README.md` or
`docs/`, so a missing file shows up as a broken image on GitHub.

Take them at your usual resolution, in game, with the overlay open. Crop to what the caption
is about rather than posting a whole 1440p desktop — a reader on a phone should still be able
to see what is being pointed at.

| File | What it should show | Used by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `recycler.png` | the recycler HUD over a full inventory, with a result showing: detected items, guaranteed yield, extra | `README.md` |
| `menu.png` | the F9 menu open over the game, root level | `docs/USAGE.md` |
| `aim-dot.png` | the aim dot settings screen, ideally with the dot visible on the game behind | `docs/USAGE.md` |
| `calibration.png` | a calibration in progress: the drawn rectangle and its grid over the inventory slots | `docs/USAGE.md` |
| `debug-panel.png` | *Show what was read* open, showing crops, the quantity strip and the three candidates | `docs/USAGE.md` |
| `crafting-tree.png` | the centred craft tree panel with its prev/next arrows | `docs/USAGE.md` |

**Before committing any of these, look at what else is in the frame.** A Rust screenshot
usually carries your Steam name, your clan tag and your server — crop them out or accept that
they are public.

PNG, please, not JPEG: these are screenshots of UI, and JPEG turns thin text into mush.
